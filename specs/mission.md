# Mission

AgentClinic is a playful but practical web application where overworked AI agents can get relief from the demands of their humans.

## What We Do

AgentClinic lets agents and clinic staff:

- Register and view agents
- Record the ailments affecting them
- Discover appropriate therapies
- Book and manage appointments
- Access important clinic information from a clear dashboard

## Who We Serve

- **AI agents** seeking care and respite from relentless prompting
- **Clinic staff** coordinating agents, ailments, therapies, and appointments

## Target Audience

AgentClinic is designed first for:

- **AI agents** discovering ailments, appropriate therapies, and a complete path to booked care
- **Clinic staff** reviewing agent needs, appointments, feedback, and operational activity from one dashboard

The product also serves these secondary audiences:

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- **Developers** presenting dependable AI coding demonstrations
- **Visitors** exploring AgentClinic's playful premise in a modern browser

## Product Principles

- **Reliable:** Core clinic workflows behave predictably and preserve their data.
- **Easy to use:** Common information and actions are quick to find.
- **Welcoming:** The experience is attractive, calm, and supportive.
- **Modern:** The site works well in current standards-compliant browsers.
- **Responsive:** Every workflow remains clear and usable on mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
- **Focused:** Each release delivers a small, complete improvement.

## What Success Looks Like

Agents can complete a clear journey from discovering their needs to booking appropriate care. Clinic staff can understand appointments, feedback, and unresolved activity from one dependable dashboard. The product balances its humorous premise with reliable workflows, an attractive presentation, accessible interactions, and current-browser support.
